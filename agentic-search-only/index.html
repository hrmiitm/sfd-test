    How to read the scores and costs

    Avg score overall and difficulty scores are mean Haiku correctness /100 over scored cases; hover over a difficulty score for its scored-case count. One case is unscored. Total cost is the known answer + judge subtotal; ≥ and hollow chart points mark unpriced attempts, so final billing may be higher. Avg time per question includes queueing for each strategy. The chart uses logarithmic known cost per planned case on X and mean correctness on Y. Hover over a strategy to highlight it; click to isolate it.

    Sources and original results are read-only. No answers were retried or rejudged for this dashboard.
