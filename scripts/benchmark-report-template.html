Experiment scope and metric definitions

Correctness is the recorded Haiku score out of 100. Cost uses recorded USD and the selected cost basis; unknown charges remain unknown. Means describe this 20-question sample.

Agentic Search in this original run could read source Parquet beyond the indexed sample. Its scores describe that wider-access system. This is not a matched-corpus comparison of ranking methods alone.

Limits of this result
  • Twenty sampled questions are a small evaluation set. Differences are descriptive, not proof of model superiority.
  • The random sample has no extreme or unanswerable questions; it cannot estimate performance in those categories.
  • Judge scores are model assessments. Review numerical answers and partial credit using the inspector.
  • Filing recall measures accessions, not whether every required fact was retrieved. Missing or failed answers are shown explicitly.
